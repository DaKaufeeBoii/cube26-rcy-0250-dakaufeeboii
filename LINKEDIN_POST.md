🚀 Excited to unveil what I built for **Round 2 of the CUBE Buildathon** presented by **@Sydon.ai** and **@CodeQuesters**!

I tackled **Track #5: Recovery Manager (RCY #5)** — *"Turn Evidence into Financial Recovery"*.

📦 **The Problem:**  
Ecommerce operators and Amazon sellers frequently receive penalties, chargebacks, and fee adjustments (packaging defect fees, carton weight variances, unplanned prep costs) weeks after the event occurs. By then, proof is scattered across disparate warehouse stations, making legitimate disputes nearly impossible to defend.

💡 **What I Built:**  
**AI Recovery Manager** — an autonomous evidence-to-recovery intelligence engine that ingests fee settlement reports and matches individual charges against operational telemetry across 5 upstream managers:
1️⃣ Receiving Manager (dock logs & pallet integrity)  
2️⃣ Prep Manager (polybagging, bubblewrap & barcode QA)  
3️⃣ Pack Manager (calibrated scales & automated DWS scans)  
4️⃣ Shipping Manager (manifests & carrier dispatch logs)  
5️⃣ Returns Manager (disposition & condition grading)  

🛡️ **Key Innovations:**  
✅ **Zero-Hallucination Guardrail**: The agent strictly enforces `SILENT — insufficient evidence` when records are missing. It never invents evidence.  
✅ **Conservative Decision-Making**: `UNCERTAIN` is a valid outcome when records conflict. Defensibility over claim volume!  
✅ **Multi-Dispute Reconciliation**: Automatically flags duplicate billing lines and cross-references past reimbursements to prevent double-claiming.  
✅ **1-Click Dispute Dossier**: Generates audit-ready dispute filing letters and Markdown dossiers for immediate submission to platform support.  
✅ **Rich Fintech Dashboard**: Built with a sleek dark-mode UI + scriptable Python CLI.
